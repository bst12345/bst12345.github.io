Atlas privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026.

Atlas is a private application used only by its owner, with his own Google account. It has no other users.

What Atlas accesses

With the owner's consent, Atlas uses the Gmail API with the read-only scope (gmail.readonly) to read his own mailbox. It cannot send, delete, label or move mail.

How the data is used and stored

  • Mail is processed on the owner's own server. Only order, shipping, receipt and personal correspondence is kept; everything else is discarded.
  • Kept messages are stored encrypted on that server and are used only to build the owner's personal notes, reminders and records.
  • Mail content is not sold, shared, or transferred to third parties, and is not used for advertising or to train general-purpose AI models.

Atlas's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
